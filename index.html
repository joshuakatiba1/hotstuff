A letter from my heart to yours

Good morning hot stuff♥️.

I really hope this message doesn't find you with a heavy heart, and I'm really sorry, my love, for making you feel taken for granted. You will always be my Evangeline 😌

In everything, I want you to know that I love you and always will 😌. You have always been the reason for the smile on my face.

I stayed up for a significant part of the night trying to figure out what to say or how I'd say it, but I couldn't figure it out, so I decided I'd just write you this letter. I hope I'll be able to relay my message to you well and not put any pressure on you.

I don't want to be the reason why you feel scared or unsafe.

Of late, I've been feeling like you are actually scared of me. That's why I've been reluctant to touch you and cuddle you sometimes. It sometimes feels like maybe I've become a scary person, and every time I think of what you told me about your past experience, I do not wish to be that person. I guess that's why I choose to always give you some space.

It's not that I'm angry at you or don't desire you anymore. I want to make you feel more comfortable and more free around me, and not have you dread my presence every time.

Sometimes I know my decisions aren't always the right ones, and I really hope that I don't make you feel neglected. I just really hope I can be better and get through to you better without having to argue or hurt you.

I really love you and want us to build more experiences together♥️.

As you read this, please don't get mad at me. I really hope it doesn't come out as cringy. Also, let me know how I can be able to love you better and how I can make you feel safer around me.

I don't want you to always be looking over your shoulder, afraid that I'm always looking to pounce on you.

Remember, I want you. Never forget that the love I have for you knows no bounds.

Hope you wake up feeling amazing and have a blast today 😌😌♥️♥️

Always yours ♥️
